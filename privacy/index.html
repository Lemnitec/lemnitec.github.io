MyGreeting privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 18 September 2026

This policy explains how the MyGreeting SharePoint Framework solution handles information. MyGreeting is published by Lemnitec.

Summary

MyGreeting runs inside the customer’s Microsoft 365 tenant. It does not send employee profile data, SharePoint content or usage analytics to Lemnitec. It has no Lemnitec-hosted backend, advertising network or third-party analytics service.

Information processed

Depending on settings chosen by the customer’s page editor, MyGreeting may process the signed-in user’s display name, given name, birthday, employee hire date, job title, department and office location. It may also read an employee record and scheduled workplace messages from SharePoint lists in the current site.

MyGreeting requests delegated Microsoft Graph User.Read to read selected profile fields for the signed-in user only. It does not request application permissions, read other users’ profiles, or write Microsoft 365 profile data.

Purpose

This information is used only to render the greeting and optional employee moments, profile details and workplace messages configured by the customer. Processing occurs in the user’s browser and through Microsoft 365 services selected and administered by the customer.

Storage

MyGreeting does not store Microsoft 365 profile data on Lemnitec systems. Optional employee data and workplace messages remain in the customer’s SharePoint site under the customer’s retention, access and governance settings.

If name correction is enabled, the corrected preferred name and first-seen date are stored in that browser’s local storage. The storage key uses the numeric SharePoint user ID and web-part instance ID. The value stays in that browser and is not transmitted to Lemnitec. Users can reset it from MyGreeting or clear their browser storage.

Customer responsibilities

The organisation installing MyGreeting controls its Microsoft 365 tenant and determines whether optional profile fields and SharePoint lists are used. That organisation is responsible for its lawful basis, notices, permissions, retention and employee-data governance. Microsoft’s handling of Microsoft 365 data is governed by the customer’s agreement with Microsoft.

Support communications

If you contact Lemnitec for support, we process the contact information and technical details you provide to respond, investigate the issue, maintain security and comply with legal obligations. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, unnecessary employee records or other sensitive data. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes and applicable legal requirements.

Cookies and tracking

These documentation pages do not intentionally use advertising cookies or behavioural analytics. MyGreeting itself does not use advertising trackers.

Sharing and international transfers

MyGreeting does not transmit Microsoft 365 profile or SharePoint content to Lemnitec or sell personal information. Information you send directly for support may be processed by the communication and hosting providers used to operate Lemnitec, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

Your rights

Requests concerning information held in your employer’s Microsoft 365 tenant should be directed to your organisation. For personal information submitted directly to Lemnitec, you may contact us to request access, correction or deletion, or to raise a privacy concern, subject to applicable law.

Security

MyGreeting relies on Microsoft 365 authentication and the signed-in user’s existing permissions. No method is risk-free; customers should keep Microsoft 365, browsers and MyGreeting current and apply appropriate tenant security controls.

Changes

We may update this policy when MyGreeting or applicable requirements change. The date above identifies the current version.
